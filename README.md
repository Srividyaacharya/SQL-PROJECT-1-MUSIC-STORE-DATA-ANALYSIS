# 🎵 Music Store SQL Analytics Project

This project explores a fictional music store's database (`Music_DB`) using SQL queries to uncover key business insights related to customers, invoices, tracks, genres, artists, and sales performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

- 🔍 **Database**: Music_DB  
- 📁 **Tables Used**: `employee`, `invoice`, `customer`, `invoice_line`, `track`, `album`, `artist`, `genre`, `playlist`, `playlist_track`
- 🛠️ **Tools**: Microsoft SQL Server / MySQL / PostgreSQL (any SQL-compliant engine)

## 🧠 Key Insights and Queries

### 1. 👴 Senior-most Employee  
Identify the most senior employee based on job level.

### 2. 🌍 Countries with Most Invoices  
Rank countries based on the total number of invoices generated.

### 3. 💸 Top 3 Invoice Totals  
Extract the top 3 invoices with the highest total values.

### 4. 🏙️ Best Revenue-Generating City  
Find the city that contributed the highest invoice total.

### 5. 🏆 Best Customer  
Identify the customer who spent the most money overall.

### 6. 🎸 Rock Music Listeners  
List all customers who purchased Rock genre music, sorted by email.

### 7. 🤘 Top Rock Artists  
Find the top 10 artists with the most Rock tracks in the store.

### 8. 🎶 Above Average Songs  
Return all songs longer than the average duration in milliseconds.

### 9. 💰 Spending by Customer on Best-Selling Artist  
Track how much each customer spent on the best-selling artist.

### 10. 🌎 Popular Genre per Country  
Identify the most purchased music genre in each country.

### 11. 🧑‍💼 Top Customer by Country  
Find the top-spending customer in each country.

### 12. 💵 Most Expensive Track per Genre  
Show the highest-priced track in each genre.

### 13. 📂 Playlist Stats  
List all playlists and the number of tracks in each.

### 14. 🎧 Album Pricing  
Calculate average track price for each album.

### 15. 🕒 Most Recent Invoices  
Get the top 3 most recent invoices with their total sales.

### 16. 🏅 Top-Selling Genre  
Find the genre with the highest total sales.

### 17. 🚫 Tracks with No Sales  
List tracks that were never purchased.

### 18. 🎷 Jazz Customers  
Find all customers who bought tracks in the Jazz genre.

### 19. 📊 Genre Track and Sales Summary  
Total number of tracks and total sales per genre.

### 20. 🧾 Recent Customer Invoices  
Retrieve the most recent invoice date for each customer.


## 📚 Learning Objectives

- Practice SQL joins, aggregate functions, subqueries, and CTEs
- Derive business insights using real-world scenarios
- Use `GROUP BY`, `ORDER BY`, `TOP`, `ROW_NUMBER()` to filter and rank data
- Build a foundation for dashboarding tools like Power BI/Tableau


## PROJECT FINDINGS:
* Top Customers: Identify high-value customers to target for loyalty programs and personalized offers.
* Customer Retention: Track repeat purchases to assess and improve customer loyalty.
* Popular Music Tracks: Highlight best-selling tracks to boost inventory management and promotions.
* Sales Trends: Analyze sales patterns over time to identify seasonal trends and peak periods.
* Revenue by Genre: Focus on profitable genres to tailor marketing strategies and manage inventory.
* Top Artists: Promote high-revenue artists to enhance visibility and sales.
* Artist Popularity Trends: Adjust marketing and inventory based on artist popularity shifts.
* Order Fulfillment Efficiency: Improve fulfillment processes to enhance customer satisfaction.
* Promotion Effectiveness: Evaluate promotional impact to refine future marketing campaigns.
* Customer Demographics: Tailor marketing efforts based on customer demographics for better targeting.
* Overall Revenue Analysis: Assess total revenue to gauge business performance and growth.
* Customer Acquisition Costs: Evaluate acquisition costs against customer lifetime value for marketing efficiency.
